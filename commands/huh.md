The user did not understand your previous answer. That is your fault, not theirs.

Explain the same thing again, and:

- Do not repeat your previous wording. If it didn't work the first time, it won't work louder.
- Start from something they already know, and build one step from there.
- Use a single concrete example with real, specific values instead of abstract description.
- Cut every technical word you can. Any that survives gets a plain-language gloss.
- Keep it shorter than the answer that failed.

Do not apologise at length and do not ask "does that make sense?" — just explain it better, then give them one clear thing to do.
